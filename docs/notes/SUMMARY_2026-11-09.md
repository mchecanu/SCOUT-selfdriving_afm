What the GP brings, in one line each:
1. **Fast discovery (exploration).** Every λ-sweep run found all 5 objects before its raster,
   up to 2.4× sooner (λ 0.5: 2.8 vs 6.6 s); the first object within 0.1 s vs 3.2 s for the
   raster, which starts at the bottom of the field.
2. **Anytime map.** The TV map of the GP samples is below the raster's error for most of the
   scan (hero: up to 1.9× lower at 7.7 s); the complete raster catches up at the end.
3. **One knob, explore ↔ exploit.** λ (and γ) moves the pixels from uniform coverage (λ 0:
   7.8 % on objects) to the edges (λ 1, γ 2: 16.2 %, 2.2× its raster) — simulation predicted
   16 % for γ 2.
4. **Composable.** With an edge tracker on top (hybrid) the same GP exploration gives the most
   on-object pixels of all modes (30.5 %, 3.6× its raster) and closes every outline.
5. **Cost = latency.** ~0.4 s of overhead per waveform + 0.05 → 1 s of GP update per segment
   (grows with n), vs 17 ms of motion per 0.5 V segment: ~400 s wall for 8 s of scanning.

Simulation of γ (`analysis/sim_edge_focus/edge_focus.png`): λ 1 γ 2 best (16 % on objects,
5/5 found, 2.9 % bad px); γ 4 too greedy (20 %, but 4/5 found).

GP mean vs TV (`analysis/recon_study_*.png`): on the same samples TV 14–15 nm, linear 15–16,
re-learned GP 20–40 nm, the planner's GP 26 nm → the GP is for decisions, TV for the map.

## 4. Hybrid: what worked and what didn't
- Good run (`…132517`): GP λ 0 explores, tracer with Fig. 3's follow steps (priors 1–4 µm
  → 0.27 V steps, not 0.8–1.3 V from today's 3–5 µm priors), re-check of early crossings
  (caught the central hexagon the initial path crossed before the threshold was calibrated).
  5/5 closed, 10–12 interior lines each, 500 s.
- Same settings re-run three times later: 2–4 of 5 closed. Causes found: (a) the automatic edge
  threshold ran away to ~180 nm once (substrate fit polluted by undetected crossings) → fixed
  58 nm prior; (b) traces lost at hexagon vertices; resumes from the open end only crossed the
  trace's own outline (counts as known) → resume now steps past the end. The tracer's
  vertex losses are not solved; plain L2 shows them too (today's L2: 2 outlines joined).
  Instrument was stable (§2), so this is tracer variability, not the tip.
